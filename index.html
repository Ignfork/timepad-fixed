HOW IT WORKS

A time stamp is a small, provable record on Zcash: one stamp, one owner, one moment.

1

Drop a file or draw a 16×16 stamp. Files never leave your browser; only their SHA-256 fingerprint is used.

2

The fingerprint is batched with others into a Merkle tree, and the root is anchored on Zcash in a transparent memo, OpenTimestamps-style.

3

Keep the .zots proof to verify the moment forever, or collect and trade stamps whole, priced in ZEC.
